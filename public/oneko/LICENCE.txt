oneko.gif
=========

oneko.gif is a 256x128 sprite sheet arranged as a 32px grid, 8 cells wide and 4
tall (32 cells total). It is black line art on a transparent background, so
src/components/oneko.tsx uses the sprite's alpha channel as a mask and fills
that mask with a flat colour. The sheet is addressed with negative cell
coordinates relative to its bottom-right cell.

Source
------
    https://github.com/adryd325/oneko.js
    The cat sprite sheet shipped with that project.
